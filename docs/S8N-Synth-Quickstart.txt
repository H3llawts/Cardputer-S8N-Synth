S8N SYNTH v0.2  /  ORIGINAL CARDPUTER

INSTALL THE MERGED IMAGE

The target is the 8 MB M5Stack Cardputer K132. Installing this build
replaces the current firmware. Save anything you need from device flash.

1. Connect the Cardputer to your computer with a USB data cable.
2. In desktop Chrome or Edge, open Espressif's serial flasher.
   https://espressif.github.io/esptool-js/
3. Connect to the Cardputer's port and check that it identifies an ESP32-S3.
   If it needs manual download mode, power it off, hold G0 while applying
   power and release G0 once the computer sees the port.
4. Choose S8N-Synth-v0.2-Cardputer-merged.bin. Set its flash address to
   0x0. Use the image's flash settings and leave "Erase all" unchecked.
   Program the image, wait for verification, disconnect and restart.

FIRST SOUND

With power off, attach ONE Unit Synth or Unit MIDI to the Grove socket.
Insert a FAT32 microSD for persistent pads, projects and microphone takes.
Unit Synth uses its own speaker. For Unit MIDI, plug into the jack marked
AUDIO. Separate / FROM CORE also sends MIDI to external gear.

Boot into Live Keys. A plays C4 on Saw Lead. The keys A W S E D F T G
Y H U J K cover a chromatic octave. Press 9 for drums. G0 or Space
silences Live Keys.

FIRST GROOVE

Press TAB, then SPACE. The 110 BPM factory pattern starts. Press 1 to 4
to switch between four variations. On the Grid, ; and . choose a lane,
, and / choose a step, and ENTER toggles the hit. M mutes the lane.
R arms live note overdub. Press SPACE to stop. TAB cycles Grid, Sound,
Sampler and Project. The backtick key returns to Live Keys.

FIRST SAMPLE

On Sampler, use , and / to select a slot. Press R, clap or speak, then
press R again. The pad stops by itself at 0.75 s. ENTER auditions it.
Pads play from the Cardputer speaker. On the Grid, choose SMP A or SMP B.
On Sound, set SLOT to your pad number. Return to Grid and toggle a step.

FIRST TAKE AND SAVE

On Project, press R to start a longer mic take. You can visit the Grid
and keep playing the external unit. Return to Project and press R to
finish. P plays the latest take. WAVs are in /S8N/TAKES on microSD.
The recorder hears the room. To capture Unit MIDI acoustically, play
its AUDIO output through a speaker. Local pads pause while the mic runs.

Project S saves your four patterns and lane settings to the selected slot.
Project L recalls it. The four WAV pads are shared between project slots.
The README in the source archive contains the full controls, WAV import
format, build commands and bench checks. Shift + / opens the key guide.
